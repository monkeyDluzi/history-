Where My Family Comes From

My mother's side came from Luanda, Angola. My dad's side is from Congo, but my dad was born in Luanda. They came together to make the Andre family, where I was born.

Stories From My Mother's Side

How My Family's Story Began

So, starting in the past, my great-great-grandmother was just a normal woman living in a village. Then my great-great-grandfather came into her life. He was charming, and eventually, they had a child together. However, my great-great-grandfather turned out to be a little too charming. He was cheating on her with many other women. Of course, she was very angry, and she told him never to show his face again. After my great-great-grandfather left, about four to six years passed. During this time, my grandmother was around six to four years old. Her father began to think about her future. He believed that because she was growing up in a village, she might never learn how to read or write, and she might not even learn how to count. He worried that one day she would become educated enough to confront him about the way he had treated her mother, and that would cause problems for him. So, he sent one of his many girlfriends to speak with my great-great-grandmother. They talked, and eventually, my grandmother went to live with her father and started going to school. However, because of her age and how little education she had received before then, school was difficult for her. She was bullied by her siblings and half-siblings because she was behind the other students. But as she grew older, things began to change. Eventually, she fell in love with my grandfather, and their story continued from there. My grandmother also had a tendency to try very hard to be liked by everyone. She always wanted people to accept her and think well of her. Although wanting to be liked isn't necessarily bad, it could sometimes cause problems for her.

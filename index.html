Family Background

My mother's side came from Luanda, Angola. My dad's side is also from Angola, but his mom's side came from Congo. They came together to make the Andre family, where I was born.

My Mother's Side

How My Family's Story Began

Generation after generation, my family's history has been shaped by unexpected events, challenges, and moments that changed their lives forever.

It all began in a small village in Angola, where my great-great-grandmother eventually separated from her unfaithful partner. Worried that their daughter—my grandmother— would grow up without a proper education, he later sent for her so she could attend school.

However, because she was behind in her studies, she became the target of serious bullying from her half-siblings and biological siblings. This experience affected her deeply and left her with a strong desire to feel accepted and valued.

Years later, her life changed because of a chance encounter. While visiting a friend from college, a young Angolan soldier walked through the door after returning from a military mission in Russia.

There was an instant connection between them, although they jokingly told each other that they were already married. When my grandfather eventually discovered the truth, he began pursuing her.

At first, my grandmother was unsure because of their nine-year age difference, but his personality and charm eventually won her over.

When my grandfather asked for her hand in marriage, her father decided to make him wait a full year before giving his blessing.

After that long wait, they were finally allowed to marry, and six months later, they became husband and wife.

Between 1981 and 1987, they had five children. Sadly, they lost their second child when the baby was only one year old. My mother, the youngest of the children, was born in 1987.

Tragically, when she was only four years old, my grandfather passed away from a heart attack.

My grandmother was suddenly left to care for four children while earning a small teacher's salary. Despite how difficult life had become, she refused to give up.

She worked two jobs, teaching at both a public school and a private school so she could provide for her family. Her determination helped keep the family together during one of the hardest periods of their lives.

Years later, another chapter of my family's story began at university. My mother was studying International Relations when she met my father, who was studying Computer Science.

They met in 2008 and fell in love. Five years later, in 2013, they got married—the same year I was born.

Following a tradition of their own, they welcomed another child every four years after that.

Now, in 2026, the cradle is still, and this is where the story of my mother's side of the family rests for now.

However, family stories never truly end. They continue with every generation, and one day, I will become another part of this story.

My Father's Side

My father's mother's side of the family comes from Congo. Although my father's family has Congolese roots, my father was born in Luanda, Angola.

This part of my family history is another important part of the story that makes up who I am.

My Story

I was born in 2013, the same year my parents got married. My story is the continuation of everything that came before me.

My family history has taught me that every generation carries stories, experiences, and traditions from the generations before them.
